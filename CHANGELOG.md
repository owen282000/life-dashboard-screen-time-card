## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- Three cards in one install: Screen Time (today on one phone, a ring against an optional daily limit, the difference with yesterday and the 7-day average, and the apps with their minutes), Screen Time Week (a bar per day against the limit, tap a day for its apps) and Family Screen Time (everyone today against their own limit, tap a person for their apps)
- Finds the phone's other sensors on its own: yesterday, the app sensors and the last sync, and the week from the integration's long-term statistics; any other sensor that resets daily works with its own statistics
- A built-in list of 126 popular Android apps with their name, color and icon, also when a source only gives the app's name; any other app gets its first letter on a color of its own
- Visual editors built on Home Assistant's own form, with per app a name, a color and hiding
- English, Dutch and German, following the Home Assistant profile
